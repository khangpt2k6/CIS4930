Paper reviews and research write-ups should read as a human reviewer speaking in the first person ("When I read Figure 6, I see...", "My main concern is", "I would add"), while keeping a professional register. No contractions, slang, or casual phrases ("cheap stand-in", "sits on top of", "worry about", "would help too").

**Why:** On 2026-10-10 the user pulled back an informal "I" draft. On 2026-11-14 they asked again for "the I or human sense" in a review (an impersonal draft felt flat), then immediately added "maintain the professional tone." Both requests stand together.

**How to apply:** Keep the Summary and Contributions section impersonal with no "I" (user asked for this on 2026-11-14: "no need I in that, be professional"). Use "I" in the judgment sections (strengths/weaknesses, improvements) for observations and recommendations. Keep word choice formal (surrogate, coincides, concerns me, I recommend). Back claims with numbers from the paper. Related: [[student-identity]], [[cis4930-team-size]].
